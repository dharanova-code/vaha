# Vaha App Icon System Guidelines

Version 1.0.0  
Status: Proposal  
Target: Vaha (Companion App Icon)

---

## Icon Design Philosophy
Vaha's app icon is the physical portal to the thought sanctuary. It must look calm, grounded, and premium among the highly saturated, gradient-heavy, and notifications-driven icons of a standard mobile home screen. It functions as a visual relief point—inviting the user to tap and return to stillness.

## Core Rules
1.  **Palette:** Limited strictly to Vaha's brand colors: Warm Paper (`#FAF8F5`) for the background, Deep Forest Green (`#1B3629`) for the main geometry, and Muted Copper (`#C07D53`) for accents.
2.  **No Gradients:** Colors must be perfectly flat, invoking the quality of screen-printed ink on matte stationery.
3.  **Silhouette Clarity:** The central symbol must remain sharp, distinct, and instantly recognizable at small sizes (down to 48px).
4.  **No Clichés:** Banned elements include AI sparkles, microphones, chat bubbles, brain waves, and technical grids.

---

## App Icon Concepts

### Direction 1: The Loop of Continuity (The Thread)
*   **Visual Structure:** Centered on a flat Warm Paper (`#FAF8F5`) tile is Vaha's *Continuous Thread* symbol in solid Deep Forest Green (`#1B3629`).
*   **Silhouette at 48px:** The loop forms a highly recognizable organic geometric knot. The high contrast of the dark forest green against the warm paper ensures it remains crisp on both light and dark mobile wallpapers.
*   **Rationale:** Speaks to a seamless thought stream. It is a single, clear path that doesn't compete for attention, appearing timeless and confident.

### Direction 2: The Thought in Stillness (The Basin & Dot)
*   **Visual Structure:** A clean, semicircular Forest Green (`#1B3629`) arc at the bottom center of the tile, with a single, perfectly circular Muted Copper (`#C07D53`) dot hovering at the optical center of the arc.
*   **Silhouette at 48px:** The contrast between the broad green curve and the copper dot creates a distinct, dual-color focal point. The copper dot serves as a warm visual anchor.
*   **Rationale:** Evokes the feeling of containment and quiet reflection. It suggests that a single thought is held safely in a quiet sanctuary.

### Direction 3: The Intellectual Lens (The Intersection)
*   **Visual Structure:** The intersecting lens shape formed by two vertical overlapping ellipses. The silhouette is rendered in Forest Green (`#1B3629`), with the inner lens detail highlighted in a fine, elegant Copper (`#C07D53`) monoline.
*   **Silhouette at 48px:** The clean, elongated leaf-like shape stands out due to its verticality, offering a premium and classic presence.
*   **Rationale:** Symbolizes focus, clarity, and depth. It represents a lens through which the user looks at their own thoughts.
