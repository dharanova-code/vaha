# Vaha Logo System Guidelines

Version 1.0.0  
Status: Proposal  
Target: Vaha (Privacy-first thought companion)

---

## 1. Logo Philosophy
The Vaha logo is designed around the principle of **Ambient Presence**. It represents a vessel for user thoughts—a quiet, grounding symbol that recedes into the background when the user is reflecting, and presents a calm, stable anchor when noticed. It rejects the hyper-active, glowing, or conversational tropes of modern AI interfaces in favor of something that feels archival, tactile, and timeless.

## 2. Wordmark Philosophy
The wordmark "VAHA" is typeset in a customized serif typeface inspired by classical stone carvings, yet balanced with clean, high-precision geometry.
*   **Case:** Uppercase only (VAHA).
*   **Details:** Terminals and serifs are sharp and delicate. The crossbar of the 'A' is dropped slightly lower than optical center to invoke a historical, book-like weight.
*   **Tracking:** Extremely generous letter-spacing (tracking +12%) to allow the letterforms to breathe, mirroring the whitespace of the application interface.

## 3. Symbol Philosophy
The symbol is a pure, abstract representation of memory, reflection, and quiet intelligence. It avoids all literal cliches (no microphones, brain waves, robot faces, or AI sparkles). Instead, it communicates the transition of spoken voice into permanent memory through geometry.

## 4. Icon Geometry
The symbol is constructed using strict geometric constraints based on intersecting circles and ellipses conforming to the golden ratio ($1:1.618$).
*   The primary stroke weight is monoline (1.5pt scale), matching the mono vector waveforms in the application.
*   Angles are aligned to 45° and 90° axes to suggest precision and structural integrity.

## 5. Safe Area
To protect the premium, uncluttered presence of the mark, a strict safe area is enforced:
*   The safe area is equal to the height of the character **'V'** from the wordmark on all four sides of the logo.
*   No other graphic element, text, or border may enter this space.

```
       +-----------------------+
       |       Safe Area       |
       |     [Height of V]     |
       |                       |
       |      [  SYMBOL  ]     |
       |                       |
       |       [ VAHA ]        |
       |                       |
       +-----------------------+
```

## 6. Minimum Size
To maintain structural clarity across physical engraving and digital viewports:
*   **Digital Viewports:** Minimum height is **16px** for the symbol alone; **24px** for the combined mark.
*   **Print / Engraving:** Minimum height is **5mm** for the symbol; **8mm** for the combined mark.

## 7. Light Theme Usage
*   **Background:** Warm Paper (`#FAF8F5`).
*   **Symbol / Wordmark:** Deep Forest Green (`#1B3629`).
*   **Accent Elements (if present):** Muted Copper (`#C07D53`).

## 8. Dark Theme Usage
Used strictly on dark slate paper textures or low-light physical packaging.
*   **Background:** Charcoal Slate (`#0F172A`).
*   **Symbol / Wordmark:** Off-White / Warm Bone (`#FAF8F5`).
*   **Accent Elements:** Light Copper (`#D9A07E`).

## 9. Monochrome / Tactile Usage
For physical expressions such as letterpress stationery, blind embossing, or laser engraving on device enclosures.
*   **Letterpress:** Solid Black ink or blind debossing on textured unbleached stock.
*   **Hardware Enclosure:** Laser-etched directly into matte natural stone-gray anodized aluminum.

## 10. Brand Expression
*   **App Icon:** The symbol sits centered on a warm paper (`#FAF8F5`) tile with zero container borders or shadows.
*   **Letterhead / Packaging:** The wordmark is blind-embossed at the top center of unbleached archival paper.

## 11. Emotional Meaning
The combination of sharp geometric paths and warm organic colors creates a feeling of:
*   **Sanctuary:** A safe, private space where thoughts are held securely.
*   **Quiet Continuity:** A thread of memory that flows without interruption.

## Logo Concepts

### Concept 1: The Continuous Thread (The Path)
*   **Visual Form:** A single, monoline forest green line that traces a smooth, looping path—crossing over itself once before resolving into a closed circle. 
*   **Meaning:** Represents a stream of consciousness, a thought captured in time, or a voice wave resolving into a permanent, secure repository.
*   **Rationale:** It evokes continuity, memory retention, and the physical act of tracing a thought.

### Concept 2: The Reflective Basin (The Vessel)
*   **Visual Form:** A forest green semi-circular arc (the basin) holding a single, solid copper dot hovering exactly at the optical center of the arc.
*   **Meaning:** The arc represents Vaha (the secure sanctuary/vessel), while the copper dot represents a single, raw thought captured in stillness.
*   **Rationale:** Inspired by Japanese garden stone basins (Tsukubai) and minimal interior design, creating an immediate sensation of containment and reflection.

### Concept 3: The Intersecting Ellipses (The Lens)
*   **Visual Form:** Two vertical, overlapping ellipses of unequal heights. The intersection area creates a quiet, lens-like shape highlighted with a hairline copper border.
*   **Meaning:** Represents the convergence of the spoken voice (temporary) and the written word (permanent), revealing a hidden center of quiet intelligence.
*   **Rationale:** Speaks to reflection, depth, and the quiet space where thoughts are refined.
